Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 31, 2026

1. Introduction & Privacy Commitment

Welcome to Anchor Launcher ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy and providing a transparent, high-performance home screen experience on Android.

Anchor Launcher is engineered with a local-first architecture. Your personal files, contacts, app drawer contents, and daily usage habits belong strictly to you. Most feature processing occurs entirely on your device, and we never sell or share user data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

This Privacy Policy complies with global data privacy frameworks, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and Google Play Console Developer Program Policies.

2. Device Permissions & Functional Scope

To operate as a full-featured Android home screen launcher, Anchor Launcher requests specific system permissions. Here is how each permission is used to deliver functionality:

📦

App Packages

QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES

Purpose: Launcher Core Exception. Required to detect, search, categorize, display, and launch installed applications from your desktop and app drawer.

Handling: 100% Local On-Device Processed exclusively on your device. Package lists never leave your device.

⏳

Digital Wellbeing

PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS

Purpose: Special App Access. Powers built-in screen time tracking, daily app usage metrics, and alert popups when custom usage limits are reached.

Handling: 100% Local On-Device All statistics and usage logs remain in private local storage.

🔔

Notification Badges

BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER

Purpose: Special Access. Reads active notification counts to display visual notification dots on application launcher icons.

Handling: 100% Local On-Device Notification contents are read solely to compute badge counts and are not saved.

📇

Contacts Search

READ_CONTACTS

Purpose: Optional Runtime Permission. Allows searching for phone contacts and quick-dialing directly from the launcher search overlay.

Handling: 100% Local On-Device Contact names and numbers are queried locally and never uploaded anywhere.

📍

Weather Location

ACCESS_FINE/COARSE_LOCATION

Purpose: Optional Runtime Permission. Fetches local geographic coordinates to render live weather updates and temperature on your home widget.

Handling: Ephemeral HTTPS Coordinates are sent directly to the privacy-friendly Open-Meteo API over HTTPS. No location history is tracked or stored on remote servers.

🎙️

Voice Search

RECORD_AUDIO

Purpose: Optional Runtime Permission. Enables speech-to-text input when utilizing the launcher search bar for hands-free queries.

Handling: 100% Local On-Device Audio input is processed locally by Android's native speech recognition service and is not retained.

🖼️

Widgets & Wallpapers

BIND_APPWIDGET & SET_WALLPAPER

Purpose: Core Launcher Features. Enables placing native Android system widgets on the home screen and updating system wallpapers.

Handling: 100% Local On-Device Managed strictly via Android system framework APIs.

4. Data Security & Third-Party Services

We prioritize security across every layer of Anchor Launcher:

  • Encryption in Transit: 100% of network data transmitted to external services (weather queries and diagnostic telemetry) is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS).
  • Encrypted Local Storage: Application settings, layout preferences, and digital wellbeing data are stored in a local SQLite database (using drift) within your device's private, sandboxed app directory.
  • No Data Selling or Advertising: Anchor Launcher contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, ad networks, or user profiling trackers. We do not monetise or sell your personal information.
  • Third-Party Service Providers:
    • Open-Meteo API: Used for fetching weather data without tracking user identities or storing location logs.
    • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics): Used to measure anonymized app performance and diagnose crash reports so we can fix bugs quickly.

5. User Control, Permissions & Data Deletion

Because Anchor Launcher is built around local processing, you remain in full control of your data at all times:

  • Revoking Permissions: You can grant or revoke optional runtime permissions (Location, Contacts, Microphone) at any time via Android Settings > Apps > Anchor Launcher > Permissions.
  • Deleting Local Data: Clearing the app cache or storage via Android Settings > Apps > Anchor Launcher > Storage > Clear Data or uninstalling the app permanently erases all locally stored database records and settings.
  • Data Deletion Requests: You may submit a data deletion request or query regarding anonymized telemetry data by emailing us at heliosbytes@outlook.com.

6. International Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under international privacy legislation (such as the EU GDPR and California CCPA/CPRA), users possess rights regarding their personal data, including the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of data. Because Anchor Launcher processes personal data locally on your device, these rights are directly actionable by managing or deleting local data on your device.

7. Children's Privacy

Anchor Launcher does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can promptly take appropriate action.
