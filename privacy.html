1. Application Identity & Privacy Commitment

Welcome to Anchor Launcher ("App", "we", "our", or "us"), provided by HeliosBytes ("Developer"). We are committed to maintaining user trust through total transparency regarding data practices, system permissions, and device security.

App Identifier: Anchor Launcher (Android Home Screen Launcher)

Developer Contact: heliosbytes@outlook.com

Anchor Launcher is engineered with a strict local-first architecture. Your personal contacts, application drawer contents, screen time metrics, and search queries belong solely to you. Feature processing occurs locally on your device, and we never sell, rent, or trade user data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

This Privacy Policy complies with global data protection frameworks (including EU GDPR and California CCPA/CPRA) and strictly adheres to the Google Play Console Developer Program Policies.

2. Device Permissions & Play Policy Justifications

To function as a default home screen desktop replacement (android.intent.category.HOME), Anchor Launcher requests specific Android system permissions. Below is the operational scope and Google Play Policy justification for each permission:

Installed App Packages

QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES

Play Policy Exception: Launcher Core Exception (Core App Functionality). Required to index, display, search, organize into custom folders, and launch installed device applications directly from your home screen desktop and app drawer.

Handling: 100% Local On-Device Package lists are queried strictly locally and are never stored or transmitted off-device.

Digital Wellbeing & Usage Stats

PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS

Play Policy Justification: Special App Access (Digital Wellbeing & Productivity). Powers built-in daily screen time tracking, application launch metrics, and limit reminder popups to assist users in curbing digital addiction.

Handling: 100% Local On-Device Usage statistics remain in sandboxed private local storage.

Notification Badges

BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER

Play Policy Justification: Special Access (App Functionality / Notification Dots). Reads active notification counts to render visual notification indicator dots on application launcher tiles.

Handling: 100% Local On-Device Notification contents are read solely to update badge counts and are not saved.

Contacts Search & Quick Dial

READ_CONTACTS

Play Policy Justification: Optional Runtime Permission (App Functionality). Allows users to search for phone contacts and initiate quick dialing directly from the launcher search overlay.

Handling: 100% Local On-Device Contact details are indexed locally and never transmitted to any external server.

Live Weather Location

ACCESS_FINE/COARSE_LOCATION

Play Policy Justification: Optional Runtime Permission (Weather Widget). Retrieves local coordinates to display accurate weather forecasts and temperatures on the home screen widget.

Handling: Ephemeral HTTPS Coordinates are sent directly to Open-Meteo API over HTTPS without user tracking or location history retention.

Voice Search

RECORD_AUDIO

Play Policy Justification: Optional Runtime Permission (Hands-Free Search). Enables speech-to-text input when using the launcher search bar.

Handling: 100% Local On-Device Audio input is processed locally by Android's native speech recognition service and is not saved.

System Widgets & Wallpaper

BIND_APPWIDGET & SET_WALLPAPER

Play Policy Justification: Core Launcher Features. Enables placing native Android system widgets on the desktop and updating device wallpapers.

Handling: 100% Local On-Device Managed strictly via Android OS framework APIs.

4. Data Security, Encryption & No-Sharing Policy

We enforce strict data security standards across the application:

  • Encryption in Transit: 100% of network traffic transmitted to external services (weather queries and diagnostic telemetry) is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS).
  • Encrypted Local Storage: Application settings, layout preferences, and digital wellbeing data are stored in a local SQLite database (via drift) within your device's private, sandboxed app directory.
  • No Data Monetisation or Advertising: Anchor Launcher contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, ad networks, or user profiling trackers. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information under any circumstances.
  • Third-Party Infrastructure:
    • Open-Meteo API: Fetches live weather forecasts without user registration, tracking, or location logging.
    • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics): Collects anonymized crash diagnostics and system interaction metrics to improve application stability.

5. Data Deletion Policy & User Rights

In compliance with Google Play Console Data Deletion Requirements, users have complete authority to manage or request deletion of their data:

  • Deleting Local Data: You can purge all locally stored settings, database records, screen time statistics, and cached weather data instantly by clearing app storage via Android Settings > Apps > Anchor Launcher > Storage > Clear Data or by uninstalling the application.
  • Revoking Permissions: Optional runtime permissions (Location, Contacts, Microphone) can be revoked at any time via Android Settings > Apps > Anchor Launcher > Permissions.
  • Submitting a Data Deletion Request: If you wish to submit a formal inquiry or request deletion of anonymized diagnostic telemetry data, please send an email to heliosbytes@outlook.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Data deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

6. International Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under international privacy legislation (such as EU GDPR and California CCPA/CPRA), users possess the right to access, rectify, or request erasure of their personal data. Because Anchor Launcher processes personal data locally on your device, these rights are directly actionable by managing or deleting local data on your device.
